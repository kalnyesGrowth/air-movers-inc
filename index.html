How We Work

Our Process

Initial Consultation

We meet at your site to understand your requirements, evaluate existing systems, and discuss your timeline and budget. No obligation, just a straightforward conversation about what your building needs.

Custom Design

Our team designs a system tailored to your building's specifications. For design/build projects, we handle engineering and planning in-house, eliminating the back-and-forth between separate firms.

Fabrication

Custom ductwork and sheet metal components are fabricated in our own shop, ensuring precise tolerances and faster turnaround than outsourced fabrication.

Installation

Our experienced crews handle every aspect of installation, from piping and controls to final commissioning. No subcontractors. One team from start to finish.

Ongoing Support

After installation, we offer preventative maintenance agreements with priority response and computerized record-keeping. We are invested in the long-term performance of every system we install.

Our Story

A Family Legacy of Quality

Founded in 1976 by the Goldberg Family

Robert and Mortimer Goldberg started Air Movers, Inc. with a clear vision: build one of the most respected commercial HVAC companies in Northern Virginia. What began with Mort handling sales while Robert and Joe managed fabrication and installation has grown into a full-service operation.

"Quality, personable service, and 100% customer satisfaction remains our mission."

From our early days focused on sheet metal fabrication, we recognized that our clients needed a single source for all their HVAC needs. We built our service department to complement our construction capabilities, creating a company that handles everything under one roof.

Today, we continue to serve the Greater Northern Virginia region with the same commitment to controlled, sustainable growth and the long-term relationships that have defined our company for five decades.
